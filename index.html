Give every tennis ball a second serve.

The Second Serve collects used tennis balls from local tennis facilities at no cost. We handle collection, sorting, and preparation before sending the balls to RecycleBalls for recycling.

Partner With Us Donate
Why it matters

Tennis balls should not have to end their journey in the trash.

Used tennis balls are easy to throw away and difficult to recycle through ordinary waste systems. We saw that problem in our own community and decided to build a simple local solution.

Our goal is straightforward: make recycling used tennis balls easy for tennis facilities, players, and communities.

Free for participating tennis facilities.

We provide collection bins, coordinate logistics, pick up the balls, and replace bins as needed. Facilities do not pay for the collection service.

How it works

Simple for facilities. Better for the game.

We take care of the logistics so a participating facility can focus on collecting balls instead of figuring out what to do with them.

01

We set up a bin

We work with your facility to determine the best collection setup and install a bin where players can easily use it.

02

Players recycle

Used tennis balls go into the collection bin instead of the ordinary trash.

03

We handle the rest

We pick up the balls whenever needed, replace the bins, and prepare the balls for shipment to RecycleBalls.
