Help every tennis ball earn a second serve.

The Second Serve is a 501(c)(3) fiscally sponsored project that aims to reduce the amount of tennis balls that end up in a landfill. We collect tennis balls from participating facilities for free. Once collected, we pack and ship them to RecycleBalls where they are processed. We operate in Southwest Michigan and are in the process of expanding within Michigan.

The Problem
400+
Tennis balls can take over 400 years to break down in a landfill.

Source: RecycleBalls / United States Tennis Association (USTA) sustainability estimates on vulcanized rubber decomposition.

01

It is incredibly easy for a tennis player to throw away their dead tennis balls. Our goal is to make it just as easy for them to recycle them.

02

Every time we visited a local park to play tennis, we saw hundreds of balls outside the fences. We needed to find a solution.

How it Works

01
↘

Collection

New facilities can participate by emailing, calling, or texting us. Once the facility has used their balls, we collect them for free.

02
→

Shipping

We count, pack, and ship the balls to RecycleBalls.

03
↗

Recycling

RecycleBalls uses their “Green Machine” to remove the outer fuzz and break down the inner rubber. The resulting product is suitable for playgrounds, horse footing, and even creating new tennis courts.
