How it works

Playgrounds, horse footing and new courts
  1. Step 1

    Collect

    A facility reaches out by email, call or text. Once their balls are used up, we pick them up for free.

  2. Step 2

    Ship

    We count the balls, pack them into boxes of 250 and ship them to RecycleBalls.

  3. Step 3

    Recycle

    RecycleBalls’ Green Machine strips the felt and grinds the rubber into material for playgrounds, horse footing and new tennis courts.

Questions

Does it cost my facility anything?

No. Pickup and shipping are free. Donations and sponsors cover our boxes and shipping labels.

What happens to the balls?

RecycleBalls removes the felt and grinds the rubber into material for playgrounds, horse arenas and new tennis courts.

Where do you collect?

Southwest Michigan for now, and we’re expanding within the state. If your facility is in Michigan, reach out.

Who handles donations?

Donations go through HCB, our fiscal sponsor, which is part of The Hack Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Run a tennis facility in Michigan?

Tell us roughly how many balls you go through. We’ll take it from there.
