Donate

If you support our mission, we encourage you to donate! Donations help The Second Serve continue to pack and ship balls for free. Donated funds help us pay for shipping, boxes, and other shipping supplies.

Support Free Tennis Ball Collection

The Second Serve is a 501(c)(3) fiscally sponsored project. Every contribution goes directly toward shipping labels, heavy-duty boxes, and packing supplies needed to send collected balls to RecycleBalls.

Donate Now

You will be redirected to HCB’s website, the organization that fiscally sponsors The Second Serve, to complete your donation.
