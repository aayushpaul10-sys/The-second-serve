Our Mission

Our Mission

The Second Serve exists to make the sport we love more sustainable. We saw hundreds of balls being littered and thrown away which influenced us to take action. In the coming years, we hope to expand to a larger portion of Michigan and make tennis environmentally friendly.

The Second Serve

A 501(c)(3) fiscally sponsored project reducing tennis ball landfill waste in Southwest Michigan.
