About The Second Serve

A local solution to a simple problem.

We saw used tennis balls being thrown away at our high school, local parks, and the YMCA. We wanted to give those balls a new life through recycling.

Our story

Built by students who saw the problem firsthand.

The Second Serve was founded in September 2026 by Portage Central High School students Aayush Paul and Ryan Fan.

What we do

We work with tennis facilities to collect used tennis balls at no cost. We coordinate the logistics, provide and replace collection bins, pick up the balls, count and prepare them, and send them to RecycleBalls for recycling.

Where we're going

We're starting in the Kalamazoo area. As the program grows, we want to bring the same simple collection model to larger communities and more tennis facilities.

The recycling process

From the court to a new purpose.

We handle the local collection and preparation. RecycleBalls carries out the recycling process after receiving the balls.

Recycle
RecycleBalls processes the balls into new materials.

The Second Serve is not an official partner of RecycleBalls. We use their recycling program to give the balls we collect a path to recycling. Learn about RecycleBalls' process.
