Partner With Us

Keep your used tennis balls out of the trash.

Any tennis center or court can work with The Second Serve to install a collection bin and create an easy recycling system for its players.

What happens next

We handle the logistics.

Once you reach out, we'll talk through your facility, estimate the best setup, and coordinate installation. After that, we handle collection and bin replacement whenever needed.

No minimum. No collection fee.

There is no minimum number of balls required and participating facilities do not pay us for collection.

Ready to get started?

Fill out our short partner form and we'll follow up to discuss the logistics.
