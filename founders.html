The Founders

The Second Serve was founded in September of 2026 by 2 Portage Central High School students who wanted to make a sport they love sustainable.

Co-Founder

Aayush Paul

Aayush leads media and public relations for The Second Serve while also collecting, counting, and shipping the balls.

Ryan

The Second Serve
