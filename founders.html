The Founders

Students turning an idea into action.

The Second Serve was founded in September 2026 by two Portage Central High School students who wanted to solve a waste problem they were seeing in their own community.

President & Co-Founder

Aayush Paul

Aayush leads media and community relations for The Second Serve while also helping manage the collection, counting, and preparation of tennis balls.

Director of Operations & Co-Founder

Ryan Fan

Ryan oversees the organization's financial operations while also helping manage collection and processing.

Why we started

We saw a lot of good tennis balls being thrown away.

We saw used balls being thrown away at our high school, local parks, and the YMCA. We wanted to give those balls a new life through RecycleBalls instead of letting them become waste.

Our goal is simple.

Make tennis-ball recycling easy enough that a facility never has to wonder what to do with its used balls.
